# 2020→2026 U.S. Senate shift-scale breakers

These are the areas where the official 2026 U.S. Senate R−D margin moved **25.00 points or more** from the 2020 U.S. Senate margin. That is the ceiling of the atlas shift legend’s “Extreme — 25+ pts” bucket. Election margins use candidate surnames: Cunningham/Tillis in 2020 and Cooper/Whatley in 2026. The shift itself uses partisan notation, so `D+26.26` means the margin moved 26.26 points toward the Democratic candidate. Other-party votes remain in each margin’s denominator, matching the atlas.

The report covers counties; congressional districts under the 2022, 2024, and 2026 line sets; and State House and State Senate districts under the 2022 and 2024 line sets.

District locations are plain-language descriptions derived from the official BEF precinct crosswalks used by the election aggregation. County membership follows the project’s ≥99.9% whole-county and >0.1% material-component rules; “most” means at least 55% of county area. Directional descriptions use the district’s position within each county. The generator validates legislative mixed districts against `data/mappings/mixed_county_components.json` before writing the report.
