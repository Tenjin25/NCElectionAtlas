# Presbyterian polling-site labels

These county-and-code labels use the display form `Church Name, DENOMINATION`. They are applied after general title casing in `scripts/build_precinct_friendly_names.js` so rebuilds preserve the denomination spelling. The affiliation belongs to the named polling-site congregation, not to voters or the precinct itself.

- **Buncombe:** The [county precinct list](https://www.buncombecounty.org/governing/depts/election/precinct-maps.aspx) identifies 06.1 at Trinity Presbyterian, whose [church site](https://trinityasheville.com/) identifies PCA. [Covenant Reformed](https://www.covenantreformed.net/about) also identifies PCA. [Kenilworth](https://pcusa.org/congregation/kenilworth-church-asheville-nc) and [New Hope](https://pcusa.org/congregation/new-hope-church-asheville-nc) appear in the PCUSA congregation directory. Oak Forest is left without a current suffix because [PCUSA records its closure in 2023](https://www.pcusa.org/sites/default/files/2024-12/2023_minutes_part_iib_stats.pdf).
- **Forsyth:** [St. Andrews](https://sites.google.com/a/standrewsnc.org/church/about) and [Clemmons](https://clemmonspresbyterian.org/about-us/) identify PCUSA. [PCUSA records Covenant Presbyterian Church at Konnoak Drive as dissolved in 2023](https://www.pcusa.org/sites/default/files/2024-12/2023_minutes_part_iib_stats.pdf), while the county still uses that building name for precinct 506. Its label is left without a current affiliation suffix.
- **Guilford:** The PCUSA directory identifies [Jamestown](https://pcusa.org/congregation/jamestown-church-greensboro-nc), [Westminster](https://pcusa.org/congregation/westminster-church-greensboro-nc), [Glenwood](https://pcusa.org/congregation/glenwood-church-greensboro-nc), [Forest Hills](https://pcusa.org/congregation/forest-hills-church-high-point-nc), and [Sedgefield](https://pcusa.org/congregation/sedgefield-church-greensboro-nc). [Salem Presbytery's directory](https://www.salempresbytery.org/find-a-congregatin) identifies Alamance and Community in Christ. [PCA's own records](https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/ga/51st_pcaga_2024_vol01.pdf) identify Friendly Hills Church as PCA.
- **New Hanover:** [Cape Fear's congregation material](https://www.capefearpres.org/) identifies it as PCUSA.
- **Union:** The [EPC church directory](https://epconnect.org/churches/) lists Benton Heights, Bethlehem, and Siler in Union County. [Bethlehem](https://bethlehemchurch.net/welcome-to-bethlehem-presbyterian/) also links directly to EPC.
